# Change Discipline Rule: Surgical Interventions

## Principles
Adapted from Andrej Karpathy and Jesse Vincent (@obra) coding standards:
- **Surgical Edits**: Change only what is necessary to fulfill the requirement. Do not reformat unrelated code, alter stylistic choices, or perform unsolicited refactorings.
- **Root Cause Focus**: Diagnose and resolve underlying root causes rather than papering over symptoms with defensive catches or temporary hacks.
- **Preserve Documentation**: Maintain existing comments, type signatures, and docstrings unless they are directly invalidated by the changes.
- **Surface Assumptions**: Explicitly state assumptions when dealing with ambiguous APIs or constraints.
- **Minimal Blast Radius**: Favor solutions with the lowest complexity and fewest dependencies that completely satisfy the criteria.
